Come and join a relaxed group of like minded people who love crafting and creating things. We meet on the first Monday of each month and are a friendly and welcoming group, for anyone.

You are welcome to bring your own project, support some of the work Oakley Stitchers have on the go, learn to knit or crochet, or have a go at something different with our make'n'take.

Look forward to seeing you.
